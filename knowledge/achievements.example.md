# Example Achievements and Claim Boundaries

## Verified outcomes

- Improved [system or workflow] through [verified action].
- Promoted from [verified role] to [verified role], if documented.

## Metrics

Record only metrics explicitly present in source evidence or provided by the user. Never infer percentages, latency values, user counts, or business impact.

## Claim restrictions

- Do not describe a contribution as leadership unless leadership is verified.
- Do not describe a service as production-scale unless scale is verified.
- Do not turn a tool exposure into expertise.
